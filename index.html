Who is Marian Stancik?
+
Marian Stancik builds autonomous AI agents for creative founders and businesses. He specializes in custom AI agent development, AI web audits (GEO and technical readiness), and multi-agent orchestration using the Hermes Agent framework. He also studies law at PF UK and builds drones as a hobby.
What does an AI GEO Audit check?
+
An AI GEO Audit checks if your website is visible to AI search engines like Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Claude, and Google SGE. It verifies llms.txt, robots.txt, JSON-LD structured data, OpenGraph tags, hreflang, page speed, and overall AI discoverability. €199 — one-time audit, report in 48 hours.
What is the AI Web Readiness Scan?
+
The AI Web Readiness Scan is a technical check by an AI agent that verifies whether your website has the required documents and structures — Privacy Policy, GDPR consent, Terms of Service, Disclaimer, AI Act disclosure, and cookie compliance. This is not legal advice — just a technical checklist. €200 — one-time scan, report in 48 hours.
Does Marian provide legal advice?
+
No. Marian studies law, he is not a lawyer. He does not provide legal advice, legal services, or compliance consulting. His AI agents perform technical checks only — they verify whether documents and structures exist, not whether they are legally correct. Clients must consult a qualified lawyer for legal matters.
