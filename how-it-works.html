How the 14-agent team works

One agent per role, one owner per task, a human decision on everything that matters. No selling — just the system.

19+ cron agents · 5+ domains covered · 3+ years building · 24/7 on EU VPS
✦ The team

14 agents, 14 roles.

Every agent has one role, one output format and an explicit list of what is not its job. Nothing runs without a human decision where it matters.

Chief of Staff

Takes a request, splits it into subtasks, delegates and quality-controls every result into one briefing.

Research

Brings only current, verifiable facts with a source link and access date — no speculation.

Legal

Works with a fixed method: facts → legal regime → subsumption → conclusion with citations. Never invents a paragraph.

Finance

Calculates only from verified documents and keeps different accounts strictly separate.

Inbox

Reads and triages new mail: needs reply / needs decision / FYI / archive. Never sends anything itself.

Calendar

Owns the schedule: free slots, conflicts, focus blocks. Moves nothing without approval.

Writer

Writes every outgoing text in one consistent voice — studied from real sent messages.

Documents

Reads contracts and long files, extracts exact facts and marks anything unreadable as [VERIFY].

Developer

Builds and maintains the code: site, scripts, integrations. Everything through pull requests.

Marketing

Produces drafts — articles, landing copy, prompts — always for approval before anything is published.

Design

Keeps the design system and prepares briefs and wireframes before implementation.

GitHub

Watches repositories, pull requests and CI; reports state and never merges without approval.

Social

Drafts posts and community updates; publishing happens only after a yes.

Support

Answers incoming questions from the inbox and knowledge base; escalates anything unclear.

✦ One day

One day in the life of the team.

08:30

Briefing

The Chief of Staff delivers the morning briefing: overnight items, today's deadlines and up to five decisions waiting — each with a recommendation.

08:45

Mail triage

The Inbox agent sorts new mail: reply / decide / FYI / archive. For replies it drafts content points — the final text only exists after approval.

09:00

Calendar check

The Calendar agent verifies that every date confirmed overnight actually exists as an event and flags conflicts. Nothing moves without a yes.

10:00

Work blocks

Research, legal analysis, documents, finance — specialists work in parallel, each with its own format: summary → proposal → what waits for a decision.

16:00

Quality control

The Chief of Staff reviews results. Weak output goes back with one concrete instruction — never a vague “try harder”. Sourced claims or it is not done.

18:00

Wrap-up

Approved results reach their destination. Everything that needs a human decision waits in one list — one question each, answerable with yes/no.
